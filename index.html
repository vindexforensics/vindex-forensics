How it works

A careful process from first contact to next steps

  1. 1

    Private intake

    Share a general description without passwords, private keys, or intimate media.

  2. 2

    Authority and scope

    Confirm lawful authority, consent, goals, limitations, and the records available for review.

  3. 3

    Preservation plan

    Identify original sources, document dates, and avoid unnecessary changes to potential evidence.

  4. 4

    Consultation and referral

    Receive organized guidance and, where appropriate, referral to legal, safety, or platform resources.
